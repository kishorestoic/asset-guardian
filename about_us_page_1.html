About Us

Our Journey to Redefine Your Home Search

FindHome started with a simple idea: to make finding a place to call home easier, more transparent, and truly exciting. From our beginnings in a single city, we've expanded across the nation, becoming the top-rated real estate app and one of the best places to work along the way.

Our Story

Our journey is marked by relentless innovation and a commitment to our users. We pioneered virtual tours and Al-powered recommendations, changing how people find homes. Today, we're proud to serve millions in over 50 cities, with a platform that's constantly evolving to meet your needs.
